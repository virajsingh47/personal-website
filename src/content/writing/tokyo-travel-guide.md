Tokyo is the most organized city I've ever visited. Not in a sterile way — in a way that makes you feel like the whole place is quietly conspiring to help you have a good time.

## Neighborhoods worth staying in

**Shimokitazawa** is where I'd stay again. Vintage shops, small live music venues, great coffee. It feels like the Brooklyn of Tokyo — except cleaner and with better ramen.

**Yanaka** is the old Tokyo most visitors miss. Low buildings, cats everywhere, a cemetery that somehow doesn't feel morbid. Great for an afternoon walk.

## Food notes

- The convenience store food is genuinely excellent. Don't skip 7-Eleven onigiri.
- Book restaurants in advance. The best spots fill up weeks ahead.
- Go to a standing sushi bar (tatami-zushi). It's fast, cheap, and often better than sit-down options.

## What surprised me

The quiet. Tokyo is a city of 14 million people and somehow, at 11pm on a Tuesday, the streets near my guesthouse were perfectly silent.
